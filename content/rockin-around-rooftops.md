Most Austinites go through December at street level: parking garages, gift lines, the long walk from the car. Rockin' Around the Rooftops asks for something simpler. Go up.

From Sunday, November 22, through Sunday, December 27, five hotel rooftops around town are running holiday pop-ups, and a paper card ties them together. Pick one up at any of the five. Order a holiday item at each stop and get a punch. Fill the card and turn it in for a $50 promo card.

That is the whole game. What makes it work is that each rooftop plays it differently.

::pair

## Naughty or Nice, on Congress

Upstairs at Caroline sits at 109 E. 7th St., on top of the Aloft and Element hotels, and its holiday theme is a question: Naughty or Nice? The crawl's own line is "pick a side on Congress at 7th." Choose your team, then order accordingly.

## Snow Much Fun, on the hour

Edge Rooftop, at the JW Marriott on East 2nd, has the gimmick that sounds like a bit until you stand under it. Real snow flurries fall every hour, on the hour. For a city that rarely sees the real thing, an hour-on-the-hour snowfall is a good stand-in for a white Christmas. Plan your order so you're holding a drink when the flakes start.

::pair

## Yule at Azul

Azul Rooftop, at the Westin on East 5th, is billed as the tallest rooftop in Austin, lit up for the holidays. Height is the point here. If you're saving one stop for the view, make it this one.

## Holiday Hideaway at Zanzibar

Zanzibar, at the Austin Marriott Downtown on East Cesar Chavez, takes the opposite approach to winter. Its pop-up is a tropical Christmas escape over Lady Bird Lake. If the snow at Edge is for people who want December to look like December, this is for people who would rather trade it for a tropical drink and a view of the water.

## Winter Sol, with igloos

The fifth stop is the one that takes planning. Otopia Rooftop Lounge, at The Otis Hotel at 1901 San Antonio St., is in West Campus, away from the downtown cluster. Its pop-up, Winter Sol, is igloos and vinyl above the neighborhood, and the igloos opened on November 22. You need it to finish the card, so it goes on the route.

::band

## How the card works

The rules are short.

- Getting a card: Ask at any of the five rooftops. Cards were available starting November 22.
- Earning a punch: Order at least one holiday menu item at a rooftop. The crawl calls it a signature holiday item.
- Order doesn't matter: Start where you like. Finish in one night or spread it over the five weeks.
- The prize: Fill all five punches, show the card at a participating rooftop, and you get a $50 promo card. It can be used from January 1 through November 1, 2027, for one visit at one rooftop.
- The fine print worth knowing: You have to be 21 or older to order alcohol, and the crawl's organizers ask you to rideshare between stops.

## Two ways to run it

The crawl's site suggests an ambitious route for people who want all five in a single night, with the stops mapped out from West Campus down to Lady Bird Lake. Any order counts, but that loop keeps the walking short. Otopia is the outlier; the other four are downtown.

The slower version is better for most people. Five weeks is a long runway. One rooftop after work on a Thursday. Another with out-of-town family over the holidays. A Zanzibar night when you'd rather be somewhere tropical. By the time the last punch lands, the card has quietly turned five ordinary evenings into a tour of the city from above.

If you'd like to pick your first stop with a little help, the crawl's site has a five-question rooftop quiz with, as it puts it, "zero wrong answers."

## Why it's worth the climb

Plenty of holiday events ask for a ticket and a time slot. This one asks only that you order a drink at a bar you might have visited anyway. The reward is real money, $50 toward a future rooftop night, earned by going out, looking around, and seeing more of Austin than you usually do.

And the reward outlasts December. The promo card is good until November 1, 2027, so a cold-week punch can turn into a warm-weather night out.

Rockin' Around the Rooftops runs daily, November 22 through December 27. Details, the route and the quiz are at rockinaroundtherooftops.com.
